Figure 2. Experimental design and paired scanning workflow. (A) Full TRIOS5 scan of the mature specimen BCG6-4 after partial cleaning and the corresponding approved region of interest (ROI). The ROI is shown at higher magnification. (B) Representative TRIOS5 ROI renderings from one fresh specimen (BCG1-2) and one mature specimen (BCG6-4). Groups G1-G2 comprised 16 independent specimens cultivated for 12 h and scanned before and after final cleaning. Groups G3-G6 comprised 32 independent specimens cultivated for 24 h and scanned at baseline, after partial cleaning and after final cleaning. (C) Each specimen-stage observation was scanned with four systems. (D) The two-stage and three-stage sequences generated eight and twelve scans per specimen, respectively, giving 512 scans from 48 independent specimens in total. Images show the exported scanner colours without colour correction. Cropping is identical across stages within each illustrated specimen. Cleaning-stage labels describe the acquisition protocol and do not imply complete biofilm removal.
