At the common 100 µm cell width, LABscanner RMS height decreased after cleaning in 15 of 16 fresh specimens and 28 of 32 mature specimens. For LABscanner, the median paired changes were −1.35 µm (95% confidence interval −2.43 to −0.85) in fresh specimens and −1.43 µm (−2.01 to −0.92) in mature specimens. Median changes for TRIOS3 were −0.23 and −0.66 µm, and those for TRIOS5 were −0.50 and −0.81 µm, respectively. Their confidence intervals spanned zero in fresh specimens and were entirely below zero in mature specimens. iTERO showed median changes of +1.51 µm in fresh specimens and −0.28 µm in mature specimens, with both confidence intervals spanning zero (Table 2).
