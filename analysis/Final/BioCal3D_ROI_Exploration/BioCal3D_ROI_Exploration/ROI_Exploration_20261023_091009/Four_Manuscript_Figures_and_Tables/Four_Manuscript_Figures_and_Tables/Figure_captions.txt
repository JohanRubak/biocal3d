Figure 4. CIELAB colour distributions across scanners and experimental stages. Panels A–F show fresh specimens (n = 16), and panels G–O show mature specimens (n = 32). Rows represent fresh baseline, fresh clean, mature baseline, mature partial cleaning and mature clean conditions; columns show L*, a* and b*. Colours identify scanners. Histograms were weighted by visible surface area and normalised within each scan before averaging with equal specimen weights. Horizontal and vertical limits are shared within each colour channel. CIELAB conversion assumes sRGB encoding, D65 illumination and a 2° observer; the distributions describe uncalibrated exported appearance.

Figure 5. Within-specimen colour changes across experimental stages. Columns show iTERO, TRIOS3, TRIOS5 and LABscanner. Panels A–D show fresh specimens (n = 16); panels E–H show mature specimens (n = 32). Thin lines follow individual specimens, thick lines show medians and shading indicates interquartile ranges. ΔE₀₀ was calculated between area-weighted scan-mean CIELAB vectors and each specimen’s own baseline; baseline change is therefore zero by definition. All panels share the same vertical limits. These are whole-specimen colour changes and do not estimate plaque coverage.

Figure 6. Mesh sampling and surface topography across scanners and experimental stages. Fresh specimens are shown in panels A–C (n = 16) and mature specimens in panels D–F (n = 32). Columns show triangle density per mesh surface area, surface-area-weighted median native face slope relative to the fitted plane, and plane-detrended RMS height after averaging into 100 µm cells. Dots represent individual scans; connected markers show medians and bars show interquartile ranges. Triangle density and RMS height use logarithmic axes, with metric limits shared between cohorts. Common cell width standardises the calculation scale without equalising scanner resolving power. These are unfiltered scanner-derived topography descriptors, not independently validated material roughness.

Rows show (A) unlit colour, (B) neutral-shaded native geometry with 20-fold vertical exaggeration, (C) native triangle edges and (D) plane-detrended height.
