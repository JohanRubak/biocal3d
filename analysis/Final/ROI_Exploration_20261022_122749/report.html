How to interpret these outputs

  • Colour: assumed sRGB, D65, 2° observer. Visible surface-area weighting within scans; equal specimen contributions to group histograms. Exported appearance, not calibrated colour accuracy.
  • Paired scanner differences match specimen and stage. ΔE₀₀ compares mean Lab colours; distribution differences are also provided. No pointwise interscanner correspondence is assumed.
  • Fresh G1–G2: baseline T0, clean T1. Mature G3–G6: baseline T0, partial T1, clean T2. Every group is included.
  • Mesh counts and edge lengths describe sampling. Duplicate coordinates at texture seams are counted separately and also geometrically deduplicated for counts. Source geometry is preserved.
  • Roughness descriptors: unfiltered, plane-detrended scanner topography. Native RMS is area-integrated; common-scale values use physical cell averaging. These are not standardised Sa/Sq measurements or independently verified physical roughness.
  • Bootstrap intervals resample physical specimens within each displayed comparison. No pixel-wise significance tests or causal claims are made.
  • Grid spacing and vertical exaggeration do not establish scanner resolution or geometric accuracy.
