## 3.2 Effect of color correction on scanner agreement and cleaning-related contrast

At baseline, color correction reduced the distance between mean RGB values, the distance between RGB standard deviations and the sliced Wasserstein distance (SWD) for all six scanner pairs in both regions defined by the original pseudo-labels. The mean pairwise reduction in SWD was 61.2% in the pseudo-label-defined ROI and 74.9% in the specimen background. Relative to the raw within-scanner reference, the between-scanner SWD ranged from 1.54–3.23 before correction and 0.74–1.08 after correction in the pseudo-label-defined ROI, and from 1.93–5.30 and 0.54–1.09, respectively, in the background (Appendix [X]).
